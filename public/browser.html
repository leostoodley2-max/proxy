Bring in a sign-in

  1. In your normal browser (not here), sign in to the site.
  2. With a cookie export extension such as Cookie-Editor, open the extension on that site and choose Export → JSON (a cookies.txt file works too).
  3. Paste it below and press Import, then reload the site's tab here.

Cookies are your signed-in session: anyone who has them is signed in as you. Only paste your own, here on your own device, and never send them to anyone.
